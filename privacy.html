Alfred privacy policy

Alfred is used only by its owner. With his permission it reads his Google Calendar and creates, changes or cancels events, and only after he confirms each change.

Calendar data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with anyone, or sent to any other service. The sign-in token is stored only on that computer, and access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's security settings.

Alfred collects no data about anyone else.
